最终纸边提示词：

> Create a portrait 1024x1536 transparent GAME UI rice-paper LEFT BORDER texture, closely matching the soft quiet edge in these reference screenshots. Produce a soft layered transition from transparent at LEFT to pale celadon paper opaque at RIGHT. The defining requirement is THREE visibly separated broad wash layers across about 220 pixels: 1 outer very faint translucent cool gray-green pigment allowing the game world to remain visible, 2 middle soft pale gray-green wash offset inward by 60-100 pixels, 3 inner clean near-flat pale celadon ivory rice paper. Their contours should gently differ so these layers are visibly separated, not stacked on the same hard torn line. All boundaries soft feathery watercolor with a few restrained irregular brush islands; NOT torn paper, NOT ragged cliffs, NOT dark heavy outline, NOT gold foil. A few tiny muted warm ochre flecks are okay but no continuous gold seam. Macro silhouette: a broad gentle smooth inward/outward drift over the full height, no sharp triangle, no point or nipple, no big sudden projection. Example overall path: outer layer around x=370 at top, gradually drifting left to x=240 through middle-upper height, gradually returning x=420 at lower quarter, then x=390 at bottom. Use broad rounded asymmetric contours, no regular sinusoidal ripples. Keep the translucent layer width broad and gentle so the paper softly grows out of the scene, not abruptly pasted over it. Rightmost 45% completely opaque very clean pale celadon rice paper #e4eadb, very subtle texture. Leftmost 15% completely transparent, actual alpha. Top and bottom extend to image edges, no frame or corners. The references supply only palette and wash-edge style; do NOT copy any UI, text, character, red annotation, screenshot scene, icons, logo, buttons or watermark.

最终整理按钮提示词：

> Generate a single game UI button background sprite, closely matching this attached reference's deep teal painted brush button. Transparent background. No text or lettering. Wide horizontal rounded brush stroke with a substantial even-height filled center and softly feathered rounded ends, pale watercolor wisps at the tips, muted dark petrol teal #285760 center. The painted body has width to height ratio about 5.5:1. It must be a plump pill-like ink wash, NOT a skinny line, NOT a sharp jagged scribble. Reference has a smooth broad center providing clear space for the words 一键整理, but DO NOT include the words in this asset. Small subdued mottling in the pigment, no gold border, no frame, no icon, no shadow outside the faint brush edge. Fill the canvas horizontally with small transparent padding; centered on a landscape canvas with true alpha transparency.

水墨导航直接复用原 InkUIFluid 与 nav-ink-blot 素材，没有重生成或替换为矩形按钮。立绘复用现有 `Appearance/portrait-{外观id}`。介绍卡背景使用共享程序渐变纹理，品质色标题向黑底过渡、底缘淡出；注意 Unity Mathf.SmoothStep 是 from/to/t 插值，透明度必须插值到 1，不能把 `.05` 当作边界参数导致整张只剩 5% alpha。

PaperWash.shader 只给纸边与干净纸面做斜向着色，保留原透明度和纸纹；右上目标色取(149,180,166)，左下浅色取(227,237,222)。Linear 色彩空间先转线性再按底纸基色归一化，最终截图约为(145,176,163)与(219,232,216)。屏幕坐标用于避开 Overlay Canvas 合批改变物体坐标的问题，两张相邻图始终使用同一纸面坐标，不出现接缝或相反渐变。
